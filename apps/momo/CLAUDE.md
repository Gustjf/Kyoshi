# Momo — weekly time budget, YNAB-style for hours (a Kyoshi app)
Rules, versioning and the app contract: the root `CLAUDE.md`. Version & changelog: `changelog.js` (continues the standalone's).

## Purpose (the original brief — keep to it; tell me if a request clearly goes against it)
YNAB-style budgeting, but for time: simple, minimalist, for organizing my thoughts. The YNAB philosophy matters most.
- A drag-and-drop card board (not a grid): a "To Be Budgeted" bank of unassigned hours from the week's 168, then Mon–Sun columns, each strictly 24h (turns red when over).
- Cards are consolidated blocks sized by duration (one 8h "Work", not eight 1h cards).
- Plan the week ahead; a baseline template auto-funds recurring commitments (sleep, work) in one click.
- Mid-week, drag cards between days as long as every day balances back to 24h.
- Weekly close-out: once a week is over, review your goals' hours (Iroh's, one row each, a goal without a card that week too): the plan counts as done, and you lower or raise what differed. The week keeps the result (`spent`), which Iroh shows as its goals' progress (goals live in Iroh since roadmap Phase 4b). Errands, jobs, meetings and the rest are marked done in their own apps (a card's "Open in <App>").
- Each app's part of the week is plain to see (roadmap Phase 8): Tasks come in a chunk per app and empty as you plan; this week's past days show what was done; the true cost (YNAB's true expenses) on the Baseline tab shows what an average week asks of each app (its own cards) against the free hours the baseline leaves, and of each block (Meeting, Iroh's goals) against what the baseline gives it.
- Each need is its own card (v4 Phase 1): an errand, a call, a workout, a meal, a cooking session, a grocery trip, a maintenance job — titled by it, sized to its minutes, with its app's icon and colour. Undated ones wait in Tasks, one each, for you to place; dated ones (meals, trips, things already done) land on their day by themselves. No app room in the baseline: generic blocks (Errands, Workout, Breakfast…) are no longer filled.
- Meetings (roadmap Phase 2): apps' meetings on a schedule fill the user's "Meeting" cards: Iroh's (its goals, monthly, each season and yearly). The other apps, Momo included, are used daily and only have a checkup (no schedule, never in Momo). Core runs them (core/meetings.js); Momo only fills the cards.
- Today (roadmap Phase 1): the phone view to run the day from — now with the time left, next, the rest of today, then tomorrow. Momo opens on it on a phone and on the board on a computer.

## State (`A.S`)
`data` { weeks, baseline, goals (old, kept), colors, asks } — shape in `model.js` · `view` "this"|"next"|"base" (the board's tab) · `today` (Today on screen instead of the board) · `undoStack`, `lastSavedJSON`, `lastSaved` ·
close-out: `closing` ({ key, rows: [{ title, hours, days, asked, done }] }), `knownToday` (Later is the store key `later`) · editors: `editing` (with `need`/`app` for an app's card), `triage` (an event's pop-up), `detail` (a card's pop-up on Today) · pointer: `press`, `stuck`, `drag` (`draw`: a card drawn from a task), `resize`, `suppressClick`, `renderPending` · `clip`, `mouse` · `ruler` · `fill` (other apps' needs in their cards, as last drawn: `shown`, `missed`) · `agenda` (the shown week's events), `agendaKey`.
Note `A.S.data` is Momo's data; `A.data` is the backup/sync adapter Kyoshi calls.

## Storage (`A.store`) and backups
Keys: `data` (plus core's `sync` and `meetings`), and `later` (the day the close-out was put off: this device's own, never synced or backed up). First open reads the standalone's `momoData_v1` (never changes it).
Backup JSON = the standalone's format, so old backups import as-is: `{ schemaVersion: 2, appVersion, weeks, baseline, goals, colors, asks }`.
A week has `events` only once you've moved one of other apps' events in it, and `spent` once its close-out logged hours; `asks` (the true cost's, by week, apart from the weeks so recording it never touches a week's cards) came in Phase 8 (shapes in `model.js`; no schema bump: older files just don't have them, and an older copy of Momo drops them, so reload Momo on every device after updating). Likewise each card's `need`, `app` and `auto` (v4 Phase 1; null / false on your own cards) and an ask's `"<app>|"` (an app's own cards): an older file's cards read as your own, and an older copy of Momo drops them.
`goals` (Momo's long-term goals from before Phase 4b) and cards' `goalId` ride along untouched: cleaned, merged and backed up as ever, never set or shown.
Every change goes through `save()` (colours, `u` timestamps, undo, then storage + `A.changed()`).

## Invariants
- Hours sit on the 15-minute grid (`snap`); one card holds at most 24h; a day is its own account (no borrowing between days).
- A day's cards are ordered; that order sets their times, except pinned cards. Nesting is one level deep (`tidyNesting`).
- Sync merges whole weeks / goals / colours by their `u` (the later change wins); goal logs from both sides are kept.
- Past weeks are closed out: only the titles of that week's `fill: "hours"` needs (Iroh's open season goals with hours in its Monday's season), in the inbox's order, each with its plan on the week's days (none without a card; Free time and events left out) and what of its ask had no card, taken as done and lowered or raised (0 to 168h) into `spent`; a past week with no goals to review closes quietly. Closing quietly and opening the pop-up wait for a settled moment (Momo shown, a new day, Confirm, Reopen): never from `A.init` (Iroh starts after Momo, and `K.inbox` leaves out apps not started yet), and after an import, a sync or another tab's save only a close-out closed elsewhere is dropped (apps' data comes in one app at a time, Momo's before Iroh's). On a phone (`isPhone`) it never pops up by itself: the banner's Review opens it, and Confirm goes on to the next. Later lasts until tomorrow on this device; with several weeks waiting, Close all as planned closes them (the one on screen as set there). This week only counts today onward.
- Cards fold, and take their colour, by title (any case): a card's old `goalId` changes neither. Another app's card (`need`) never folds, nor is it any card's twin (group drag, the editor's days), and every card with an `app` (a pasted copy too) takes its app's colour (`a:<app>`: `APP_COLORS` first while no key on show has it; a task of it shows that before any card does; the editor's swatches change all of that app's cards). No colour is let go before every app has started (`K.ready`), so starting Momo never drops other apps' events' colours and saves them back.
- **Apps' own cards** (Momo decides when, each app what): a `fill: "card"` need (core/inbox.js) is one card of its own, `need: "<app>:<id>"`, titled by it (`cleanText`), sized to its minutes (60 when it says none), with its app's icon. It goes on the card keyed to it, else (a done one) the card keyed to its `of` — any day this week and next, one need a card, dated needs first, the one on its day first, else the soonest (`assign`); nothing else fills that card. Without one: open and undated, a task of its own (drag it onto a day, or click it and pick one day: it draws that card); with a day, `place.js` makes it (`auto: true`), on that day as near its `time` as the day's cards allow (before a card, in the middle of one on its own that isn't an app's, or at the end; no time: the end), only in a week with a card of yours on a day (an empty week isn't planned yet: its dated needs wait in Tasks until Load baseline or Copy previous week, which place them; Load baseline places Momo's untouched ones again among the fresh copies), never in a closed one; done and dated, or dated on a day gone by, never to Tasks. While `auto`, a card follows its need's title and minutes, and once it holds nothing it's taken back if it's on today or later (past days keep theirs: the record of the week). Moving, resizing, pinning, cutting and pasting, or editing a card in its pop-up turns `auto` off: then it stays, need or no need. `placeCards` runs only with `K.ready` and Momo on screen (onShow, every onTick, onReload, Load baseline, Copy previous week), never from `A.init`; its changes are a quiet save, `undo: false` (synced like any change). A card whose need has a day of its own (`isDated`: a meal, a trip, something done) has no Delete ("Change it in <App>"), ignores Alt+click and can't go into Tasks; another app's card dropped into Tasks is deleted, its need a task again. A card on a day gone by holding an open need without a day is missed: red-edged, its label says so. Copies (Ctrl+C) keep `app`, not `need`; Copy previous week and Save as baseline leave apps' cards out (no app room in the baseline).
- **Blocks**: the other needs aren't stored either; like the apps' own, they're read afresh each draw (`K.inbox`, from this Monday to next Sunday), read-only. A block is any card on a day this week and next (baseline-loaded and closed weeks' too) that isn't an app's own, soonest first by day then start time. A need fills the soonest block titled its `block` (any case): those with a `date` first, on that day, a past day's too (done there: the record of the week); one with a `due`, on or before it (once overdue, any), and not before its `from` if it has one; the rest in their app's order (a sequence); every one without a `date` from today on. A card holding a late need (not done; overdue or past its due) is red-edged, and its label, pop-ups and Today's row say late. Apps' meetings (core/meetings.js: Iroh's) come last, each filling a card titled "Meeting" in the week before it's due (`from`), any marked `after` after the others'. "block" needs take an empty block each; "time" ones go in whole while the card's hours have room (no length: 60m); "ongoing" ones show on every block with their title; "hours" ones (Iroh's goals, a need a week from its Monday) spread over the blocks with their title in turn, each taking the room it has, after the cards with that title on the week's days before today, which count as done (the plan is taken as done). So moving, resizing or pasting cards just works, and filling adds no hours and no conflict. A done need shows ✓ and never goes to Tasks. "Open in <App>" closes the editor, then `K.inbox.open`.
- Tasks aren't stored either: what no card covers and the ongoing needs, in a chunk per app (switcher order), each with how many to place and how long. A week's board lists only what can go on it (`canGo`: its day's week; an hours need's own week; else from its `from` (today at the soonest) to its `due`, any day once that's passed), so something that could go on either shows on both. An app's need for a card of its own is a task each (`need`, in its app's colour); a timed shortfall is one task per app and block title, as long as all of it (rounded up to 15m); an hours need's is one per week; a whole block's need is a task each; ongoing ones are never used up. A drawn card is an ordinary card (an app's holding its need), parked until it moves onto a day. A card without a day (`day: null`, "parked") sits in Tasks ("No day"); an app's card never does. A week is "all assigned ✓" (its tab, and the bank's "Every hour has a job ✓") only once every hour has a job and its Tasks (but the ongoing) and parked cards are empty (`toPlace`). The baseline has no parked cards: its Tasks are the true cost and the ongoing needs.
- The true cost (`truecost.js`): while a week is this week, what the apps ask of it by app and block title — `"<app>|"` for an app's own cards — (minutes, done ones too, never the ongoing) is kept in `data.asks`, recorded once every app has started (`K.ready`) and only when this device sees it change (an import sees it afresh), so two devices never write over each other in turn; a quiet save (Export JSON stays as it was). Its average over the weeks kept among the last `COST_WEEKS` (13, this one too): each app's own cards, together, against the free hours the baseline leaves (168h less its cards, Free time counting as free) — one line on the baseline's Tasks, ✓ or short (amber), and the baseline's bank says so when short; an app asking for cards of its own (now, or in a week kept) and nothing else now has its block titles kept from before folded into its own; each block title against the baseline's cards with that title: a block it's short of is a task as long as the gap, one it covers a ✓.
- Events from other apps aren't stored: they're read afresh (`K.agenda`), and only the times you moved them to on their own day are kept (`week.events`, by "app:id"). Moving one never changes its app, and cards never move for one.
- An event at a time counts toward its day's 24 hours (one at any time of day, like a birthday, doesn't): it takes time no card has, or Free time lends it the hours. Anywhere else it conflicts (with those cards, and any event it overlaps), from today onward; a conflict with no clear time within `EVENT_WINDOW` (3) hours of its app's time stays flagged. It never moves further than that, nor to another day.
- Today stores nothing and changes nothing itself: it reads the board (today and tomorrow, which may be next week) and draws it as a list. Its pop-ups that change things are the board's (card editor, an event's), so they first put the board's tab on that card's week (`S.view`).
- Bug reports never include card titles, goal names or what events are.

## Shared with other apps
`A.hoursSpent(title)` (`closeout.js`; Iroh reads it, roadmap Phase 4b): `{ "<Monday>": hours }` for every closed week: the hours
its close-out logged for that title (any case), 0 when it had none. Since Momo 6.964 a week's `spent` holds only the goals' titles (4b's
close-outs logged every title), so any other title is 0 there. A week closed without that review (quietly, with no goals; before Phase
4b, by an older copy, or from an old backup) counts its plan. A week never planned (no record kept: a holiday), from the first week Momo
kept to last week, is 0 (Phase 8: the goals fall behind for it, honestly). A fresh object on every call, so it's read-only.
