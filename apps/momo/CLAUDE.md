# Momo — weekly time budget, YNAB-style for hours (a Kyoshi app)
Rules, versioning and the app contract: the root `CLAUDE.md`. Version & changelog: `changelog.js` (continues the standalone's).

## Purpose (the original brief — keep to it; tell me if a request clearly goes against it)
YNAB-style budgeting, but for time: simple, minimalist, for organizing my thoughts. The YNAB philosophy matters most.
- A drag-and-drop card board (not a grid): a "To Be Budgeted" bank of unassigned hours from the week's 168, then Mon–Sun columns, each strictly 24h (turns red when over).
- Cards are consolidated blocks sized by duration (one 8h "Work", not eight 1h cards).
- Plan the week ahead; a baseline template auto-funds recurring commitments (sleep, work) in one click.
- Mid-week, drag cards between days as long as every day balances back to 24h.
- Weekly close-out: once a week is over, review your goals' hours (Iroh's, one row each, a goal without a card that week too): the plan counts as done, and you lower or raise what differed. The week keeps the result (`spent`), which Iroh shows as its goals' progress (goals live in Iroh since roadmap Phase 4b). Errands, jobs, meetings and the rest are marked done in their own apps (a card's "Open in <App>").
- Each app's part of the week is plain to see (roadmap Phase 8): Tasks come in a chunk per app and empty as you plan; this week's past days show what was done; the true cost (YNAB's true expenses) on the Baseline tab shows what an average week asks of each app (its own cards) against the free hours the baseline leaves, and of each block (Meeting, Iroh's goals) against what the baseline gives it.
- Each need is its own card (v4 Phase 1): an errand, a call, a workout, a meal, a cooking session, a grocery trip, a maintenance job — titled by it, sized to its minutes, with its app's icon and colour. Undated ones wait in Tasks, one each, for you to place; dated ones (meals, trips, things already done) land on their day by themselves. No app room in the baseline: generic blocks (Errands, Workout, Breakfast…) are no longer filled.
- Meetings (roadmap Phase 2): apps' meetings on a schedule fill the user's "Meeting" cards: Iroh's (its goals, monthly, each season and yearly). The other apps, Momo included, are used daily and only have a checkup (no schedule, never in Momo). Core runs them (core/meetings.js); Momo only fills the cards.
- Today (roadmap Phase 1): the phone view to run the day from — now with the time left, next, the rest of today, then tomorrow. Momo opens on it on a phone and on the board on a computer.
- Upcoming weekends (v4 Phase 2), so weekends get spent on purpose: a folded line under the board and under Today says how many of the next 13 weekends have a plan; open, each weekend with its brief plan (or "No plan"), to type one. A plan is just a note: it takes no hours. The board and Today stay the focus.
- The apps' routines (v4 Phase 7): what happens at the same times every week (Turtleduck's breakfast, lunch and dinner, its scheduled grocery trips) has a card of its own in the baseline, pinned at the time set in that app, kept by Momo; a week's meals fill those slots once it's loaded. Cards an app sets (its slots, a cooking session at its time) are fixed here: changed in that app only.

## State (`A.S`)
`data` { weeks, baseline, goals (old, kept), colors, asks, weekends } — shape in `model.js` · `view` "this"|"next"|"base" (the board's tab) · `today` (Today on screen instead of the board) · `undoStack`, `lastSavedJSON`, `lastSaved` ·

Note `A.S.data` is Momo's data; `A.data` is the backup/sync adapter Kyoshi calls.

## Storage (`A.store`) and backups
Keys: `data` (plus core's `sync` and `meetings`), and `later` (the day the close-out was put off: this device's own, never synced or backed up). First open reads the standalone's `momoData_v1` (never changes it).
Backup JSON = the standalone's format, so old backups import as-is: `{ schemaVersion: 2, appVersion, weeks, baseline, goals, colors, asks, weekends }`.
A week has `events` only once you've moved one of other apps' events in it, and `spent` once its close-out logged hours; `asks` (the true cost's, by week, apart from the weeks so recording it never touches a week's cards) came in Phase 8 (shapes in `model.js`; no schema bump: older files just don't have them, and an older copy of Momo drops them, so reload Momo on every device after updating). Likewise each card's `need`, `app` and `auto` (v4 Phase 1; null / false on your own cards) and an ask's `"<app>|"` (an app's own cards): an older file's cards read as your own, and an older copy of Momo drops them. And `weekends` (v4 Phase 2): an older file has no plans, and an older copy of Momo drops them. And each card's `slot` and `fixed` (v4 Phase 7; null / false on your own cards, kept only on an app's: `slot` with its app's prefix): an older file has none, and an older copy of Momo drops them (so reload Momo on every device; a reloaded copy makes the slot cards again).
`goals` (Momo's long-term goals from before Phase 4b) and cards' `goalId` ride along untouched: cleaned, merged and backed up as ever, never set or shown.
Every change goes through `save()` (colours, `u` timestamps, undo, then storage + `A.changed()`).

## Invariants
- Hours sit on the 15-minute grid (`snap`); one card holds at most 24h; a day is its own account (no borrowing between days).
- A day's cards are ordered; that order sets their times, except pinned cards. Nesting is one level deep (`tidyNesting`).
- Every card on a day shows when it starts and ends (board and Today; other apps' events at a time too): an inner card and a card on its own from its start for its hours; a block's own part from its start to where the cards at its bottom begin (around those in its middle); a pinned card's pin is when its whole block starts. On the board an end that doesn't fit beside the hours is left out (`fitClocks`), never the start or the hours.
- **Before & after** (`sides.js`, report 2): nothing new is stored. A card's before and after are ordinary cards inside it (`parentId`, `pos: "top"` / `"bottom"`) titled as the editor says (default "Commute"), one at each end, the minutes each (0 = none, the default; up to `SIDE_MAX`, 15-minute steps); Save sets them on the card and each copy or twin it's put on (`setSides`, after any fold: the card it became), renames those cards when their title changes, and leaves the others inside it (a Lunch in its middle, another title) alone. The editor offers them for a card that can hold cards (not set in its app, not inside or going inside another; a parked one too); the "discard changes?" snapshot and `auto` (edited: yours) count them.
- Sync merges whole weeks / goals / colours / weeks' asks / weekends' plans by their `u` (the later change wins); goal logs from both sides are kept.
- Past weeks are closed out: only the titles of that week's `fill: "hours"` needs (Iroh's open season goals with hours in its Monday's season), in the inbox's order, each with its plan on the week's days (none without a card; Free time and events left out) and what of its ask had no card, taken as done and lowered or raised (0 to 168h) into `spent`; a past week with no goals to review closes quietly. Closing quietly and opening the pop-up wait for a settled moment (Momo shown, a new day, Confirm, Reopen): never from `A.init` (Iroh starts after Momo, and `K.inbox` leaves out apps not started yet), and after an import, a sync or another tab's save only a close-out closed elsewhere is dropped (apps' data comes in one app at a time, Momo's before Iroh's). On a phone (`isPhone`) it never pops up by itself: the banner's Review opens it, and Confirm goes on to the next. Later lasts until tomorrow on this device; with several weeks waiting, Close all as planned closes them (the one on screen as set there). This week only counts today onward.
- Cards fold, and take their colour, by title (any case): a card's old `goalId` changes neither. Another app's card (`need`) or a slot's (`slot`) never folds, nor is it any card's twin (group drag, the editor's days), and every card with an `app` (a pasted copy too) takes its app's colour (`a:<app>`: `APP_COLORS` first while no key on show has it; a task of it shows that before any card does; the editor's swatches change all of that app's cards). No colour is let go before every app has started (`K.ready`), so starting Momo never drops other apps' events' colours and saves them back.
- **Apps' own cards** (Momo decides when, each app what): a `fill: "card"` need (core/inbox.js) is one card of its own, `need: "<app>:<id>"`, titled by it (`cleanText`), sized to its minutes (60 when it says none), with its app's icon. It goes on its slot's card on its date (one naming a slot: Slots, below), else the card keyed to it, else (a done one) the card keyed to its `of` — any day this week and next, one need a card, dated needs first, the one on its day first, else the soonest (`assign`); nothing else fills that card. Without one: open and undated, a task of its own (drag it onto a day, or click it and pick one day: it draws that card); with a day, `place.js` makes it (`auto: true`; a `fixed` one pinned at its time in any open week: Cards set in their app, below), on that day as near its `time` as the day's cards allow (before a card, in the middle of one on its own that isn't an app's, or at the end; no time: the end), only in a week with a card of yours on a day (an empty week isn't planned yet: its dated needs wait in Tasks until Load baseline or Copy previous week, which place them; Load baseline places Momo's untouched ones again among the fresh copies), never in a closed one; done and dated, or dated on a day gone by, never to Tasks. While `auto`, a card follows its need's title and minutes, and once it holds nothing it's taken back if it's on today or later (past days keep theirs: the record of the week). Moving, resizing, pinning, cutting and pasting, or editing a card in its pop-up turns `auto` off: then it stays, need or no need. `placeCards` runs only with `K.ready` and Momo on screen (onShow, every onTick, onReload, Load baseline, Copy previous week), never from `A.init`; its changes are a quiet save, `undo: false` (synced like any change). A card whose need has a day of its own (`isDated`: a meal, a trip, something done) has no Delete ("Change it in <App>"), ignores Alt+click and can't go into Tasks; another app's card dropped into Tasks is deleted, its need a task again. A card on a day gone by holding an open need without a day is missed: red-edged, its label says so. Copies (Ctrl+C) keep `app`, not `need`; Copy previous week and Save as baseline leave apps' cards out (no app room in the baseline).
- **Slots** (`routine.js`): each slot of an app's routine (`K.routine`, core/routine.js; Turtleduck's breakfast, lunch and dinner each day, its scheduled trips) is a card in the baseline, `slot: "<app>:<id>"`, `app`, `auto` and `fixed`: on its day, pinned at its time (`timeSpot`: before the first card starting at or after it, your cards left where they were, so one running into it is flagged by `timeTrouble`, for you to arrange once), titled by it, as long as it says. `syncSlots` keeps them so (a slot gone taken back, a second card for one too; a new one added; a changed one updated and put back at its time), matched by `slot`, never by id, writing only on a difference and in key order, so two devices that know the same routine converge (the baseline merges whole by `u`). It runs first in `placeCards` (so only with `K.ready` and Momo on screen: never from `A.init`, when the routines are still empty and every slot card would go), after Save as baseline (which leaves slot cards out) and after Clear (which keeps them). Load baseline and Copy previous week bring the slot cards into a week as copies (still `slot`, `fixed`, `auto`); there the need naming a slot (core/inbox.js `slot`) fills its slot's card on its date before any other card (`assign`: so a card placed for it before it had a slot holds nothing and goes), and the card takes the need's title, minutes and time (a day's exception); empty again, it follows its slot; a copy whose slot is gone is taken back from today on, and one whose slot lasts never is (it's the day's slot). Closed weeks are never touched. `hasData` leaves out slot cards and the cards Momo placed (`auto`), so a new device that just made them still takes a sync folder's data whole (core/sync.js), and an import of nothing but those is refused.
- **Cards set in their app** (`fixed`: a slot's card, a card Momo placed for a `fixed` need): a fixed need with a day is placed pinned at its `time` (`timeSpot`) in any open week this week or next, one not planned yet too (the week record is made: `isPlanned` still says not planned, as the bank's "Start with your baseline" and the tab do), never in a closed one, and never goes to Tasks. Such a card follows its need's time as well (moved back to it with `putAt`). It can't be dragged (a mouse drag wiggles its pin, a touch opens it), resized (no grip), pinned or unpinned (its pin is only a sign, shown on phones too), cut (Ctrl+X wiggles its pin), deleted (no Delete, Alt+click ignored, never dropped into Tasks: `removeCard` refuses it), nor can a card go inside it (`canHold`, the editor's Inside list); its pop-up is read-only (Close, "Change it in <App>" at what it holds, or "Set in <App>" at an empty slot's routine id), and Today's has no Edit (`#detailChange`). Its colour, the app's, still changes from its pop-up, at once. A copy (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) is yours: `app` kept, `slot`, `fixed`, `need` and `auto` not. Clear keeps every fixed card; Undo is followed by `placeCards`, so what's set in other apps heals at once.
- **Blocks**: the other needs aren't stored either; like the apps' own, they're read afresh each draw (`K.inbox`, from this Monday to next Sunday), read-only. A block is any card on a day this week and next (baseline-loaded and closed weeks' too) that isn't an app's own nor a slot's, soonest first by day then start time. A need fills the soonest block titled its `block` (any case): those with a `date` first, on that day, a past day's too (done there: the record of the week); one with a `due`, on or before it (once overdue, any), and not before its `from` if it has one; the rest in their app's order (a sequence); every one without a `date` from today on. A card holding a late need (not done; overdue or past its due) is red-edged, and its label, pop-ups and Today's row say late. Apps' meetings (core/meetings.js: Iroh's) come last, each filling a card titled "Meeting" in the week before it's due (`from`), any marked `after` after the others'. "block" needs take an empty block each; "time" ones go in whole while the card's hours have room (no length: 60m); "ongoing" ones show on every block with their title; "hours" ones (Iroh's goals, a need a week from its Monday) spread over the blocks with their title in turn, each taking the room it has, after the cards with that title on the week's days before today, which count as done (the plan is taken as done). So moving, resizing or pasting cards just works, and filling adds no hours and no conflict. A done need shows ✓ and never goes to Tasks. "Open in <App>" closes the editor, then `K.inbox.open`.
- Tasks aren't stored either: what no card covers and the ongoing needs, in a chunk per app (switcher order), each with how many to place and how long. A week's board lists only what can go on it (`canGo`: its day's week; an hours need's own week; else from its `from` (today at the soonest) to its `due`, any day once that's passed), so something that could go on either shows on both. An app's need for a card of its own is a task each (`need`, in its app's colour); a timed shortfall is one task per app and block title, as long as all of it (rounded up to 15m); an hours need's is one per week; a whole block's need is a task each; ongoing ones are never used up. A drawn card is an ordinary card (an app's holding its need), parked until it moves onto a day. A card without a day (`day: null`, "parked") sits in Tasks ("No day"); an app's card never does. A week is "all assigned ✓" (its tab, and the bank's "Every hour has a job ✓") only once every hour has a job and its Tasks (but the ongoing) and parked cards are empty (`toPlace`). The baseline has no parked cards: its Tasks are the true cost and the ongoing needs.
- The true cost (`truecost.js`): while a week is this week, what the apps ask of it by app and block title — `"<app>|"` for an app's own cards, but a need filling a slot under its slot's title (its `block`: "Dinner") — (minutes, done ones too, never the ongoing) is kept in `data.asks`, recorded once every app has started (`K.ready`) and only when this device sees it change (an import sees it afresh), so two devices never write over each other in turn; a quiet save (Export JSON stays as it was). Its average over the weeks kept among the last `COST_WEEKS` (13, this one too): each app's own cards, together, against the free hours the baseline leaves (168h less its cards, Free time counting as free) — one line on the baseline's Tasks, ✓ or short (amber), and the baseline's bank says so when short; an app asking for cards of its own (now, or in a week kept) and nothing else now has its block titles kept from before folded into its own, but not a title of its slot cards in the baseline (a slot row: its room is those cards, already out of the free time, so nothing counts twice; when short, its words say to give its slots a longer usual length in that app, or to drag the gap in); each block title against the baseline's cards with that title: a block it's short of is a task as long as the gap, one it covers a ✓.
- Events from other apps aren't stored: they're read afresh (`K.agenda`), and only the times you moved them to on their own day are kept (`week.events`, by "app:id"). Moving one never changes its app, and cards never move for one.
- An event at a time counts toward its day's 24 hours (one at any time of day, like a birthday, doesn't): it takes time no card has, or Free time lends it the hours. Anywhere else it conflicts (with those cards, and any event it overlaps), from today onward; a conflict with no clear time within `EVENT_WINDOW` (3) hours of its app's time stays flagged. It never moves further than that, nor to another day.
- **Weekends** (`weekends.js`): a plan per weekend, by its Saturday (`data.weekends`, apart from the weeks: a week's sync, close-out, true cost and 168h never see it), one line of at most `PLAN_MAX` (120) characters, through `save()` (undo, sync, backups). Clearing one keeps it as `""` with its time, never deleting the key, so the clearing wins over an older copy's plan when two devices combine; an empty Save clears too. Past weekends' plans stay, unshown. The folds show this weekend (on a Saturday or Sunday the one it's in, else the coming one; time travel aware) and the 12 after it; each stays open or closed as you leave it until the page reloads (closed at first). The board's Saturday heading, this week's and next week's (not the baseline's), shows its weekend's plan (tap: the pop-up, a closed week's too); the other days get an empty line as tall, so every day's cards start level. Bug reports count plans, never their words.
- Today stores nothing and changes nothing itself: it reads the board (today and tomorrow, which may be next week) and draws it as a list. Its pop-ups that change things are the board's (card editor, an event's), so they first put the board's tab on that card's week (`S.view`).
- Bug reports never include card titles, goal names or what events are.

## Shared with other apps
`A.hoursSpent(title)` (`closeout.js`; Iroh reads it, roadmap Phase 4b): `{ "<Monday>": hours }` for every closed week: the hours
its close-out logged for that title (any case), 0 when it had none. Since Momo 6.964 a week's `spent` holds only the goals' titles (4b's
close-outs logged every title), so any other title is 0 there. A week closed without that review (quietly, with no goals; before Phase
4b, by an older copy, or from an old backup) counts its plan. A week never planned (no record kept: a holiday), from the first week Momo
kept to last week, is 0 (Phase 8: the goals fall behind for it, honestly). A fresh object on every call, so it's read-only.

`A.weekStatus(monday, app = null)` (`routine.js`; Turtleduck reads it, v4 Phase 7): a week as Momo has it, any day of it will do:
`{ exists, planned (a card on a day besides those Momo placed for the apps' needs: isPlanned), closed, slots }`, `slots` being the routine
ids of `app`'s slot cards on its days ("dinner:3"; every app's "<app>:<id>" when `app` is null); `{ exists: false, planned: false, closed:
false, slots: [] }` for a week Momo has no record of (or no data yet). A fresh object on every call.
