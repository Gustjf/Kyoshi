## Context

Today Turtleduck tells Momo what each meal, cooking session and grocery trip is, with time hints baked into `apps/turtleduck/share.js` (`TIMES`, line 17: 07:30 / 12:00 / 18:00 / cook 16:00 / trip 10:00). Momo places each as a card of its own "as near its time as the day's cards allow" (`apps/momo/place.js` — never pinned, so the shown time can be an hour or more off), and in two cases the owner must drag Turtleduck's things out of Momo's Tasks strip: a week with no cards of yours yet (`place.js:88`) and the undated "Now" grocery list (`share.js:72–81`). The owner wants the normal eating times, cooking time and a weekly trip schedule set **in Turtleduck** (with a per-day exception by tapping the meal); meals and trips **in Momo's baseline** at exact times, since they're predictable; a week's meals on Momo only once that week is **confirmed** in Turtleduck; **nothing of Turtleduck's in Momo's Tasks**; an indicator of whether the shopping happens **before** each meal is cooked; and the root rule "apps never touch each other's data" replaced by "apps may talk through functions they offer".

## Decisions (the owner's answers this session; don't reopen)

1. **Apps talking:** an app may read another and ask it to change things, but only through a function that app offers (so its own save, undo and sync run); never its `A.S` or storage; no "ask me first".
2. **What gets times:** breakfast, lunch, dinner, the Cook row, grocery trips. Snacks stay out of Momo.
3. **After confirming,** later changes flow through within a minute (the week stays confirmed).
4. **The Now list stays out of Momo**; its meals get the coverage mark instead.
5. **Trips get a weekly schedule** ("every Sunday 8:00 AM, every Tuesday 6:00 PM"); one-off trips keep a usual time.
6. **Turtleduck feeds Momo's baseline:** Momo reads Turtleduck's routine and keeps the slot cards there itself, following changes.
7. **Turtleduck's cards are fixed in Momo:** no drag, resize, pin, delete or cut; "Change it in Turtleduck".
8. **Exact, pinned** times on Momo's board; the owner arranges the other baseline cards around them once; a card running into a pinned one is flagged as Momo already does.
9. **No daily Cook slot:** a cooking session is placed on its own, pinned, only on days the Cook row is used.
10. **Coverage by time within the day** (a Tuesday 6 pm trip doesn't cover Tuesday's 4 pm cooking or 6 pm dinner).
11. **Dot** on Turtleduck's icon from Friday while next week isn't confirmed, and any day while this week isn't (once in use).
12. **Tests, straight to `main`** (the root rules apply again: refresh the stale test, add tests, `node tests/run.js` green).
13. **"Times & trips" pop-up** from the Plan's ⋯ menu and from Groceries → Settings.
14. **A filled slot takes the meal's own minutes**; the slot's usual length applies while it's empty.
15. **Turtleduck reads Momo back** and shows under the week's tab whether that week has its slots there.
16. **Confirm warns about gaps** (days with no dinner, meals with no trip before them) and confirms anyway.

## The shape

```
Turtleduck  A.routine()  ──► core K.routine ──► Momo baseline: one pinned slot card per (meal, weekday) and per scheduled trip day
Turtleduck  A.inbox()    ──► core K.inbox   ──► Momo weeks: a confirmed week's meal fills its slot card (same weekday) at its time;
            (confirmed weeks only; needs carry slot, time, fixed)   cook sessions and off-schedule trips: a pinned card of their own
Momo        A.weekStatus(monday, app)  ◄── Turtleduck's line "On Momo ✓" / "its week has no baseline yet"
```
Weekdays are 0 = Monday … 6 = Sunday in both apps (Momo `DAYS`; Turtleduck `mondayOf`). Sunday's trip slot is `groceries:6`. All times sit on Momo's 15-minute grid.

## Risks and how the plan avoids them
- **Two devices and the baseline slots:** match by `slot`, write only on a difference, deterministic order; the baseline merges whole by `u`, so once both devices have Turtleduck's settings they converge (`dataKey` equal, nothing to write). A concurrent hand edit of the baseline on the other device can still lose to the later stamp (today's rule).
- **A fresh device's empty baseline beating the folder's:** the `hasData` change (Momo) — slot and auto cards don't count as data.
- **K.order / K.ready:** Momo starts before Turtleduck; `syncSlots` and `placeCards` keep the `K.ready` guard and never run from `A.init`; `isFixed` and the true cost read stored card fields, not the routine, so the first render is right; Turtleduck reads `weekStatus` only through the guarded call.
- **Closed weeks:** never placed into or synced; their slot copies stay as the record (locked anyway).
- **Load baseline / Copy previous week / Clear / Save as baseline / Undo:** covered in Push 1 (fresh copies, slots carried, heal-at-once, slots left out and re-added, placeCards after undo).
- **True cost double counting:** slot needs under the slot title (room = the slot cards; `busy` already counts them); only cook sessions and extra trips against free hours.
- **Ids:** need `meal:<date>:<meal>` / `cook:<date>` / `groceries:<date>`; routine `dinner:3`; card `slot` `"turtleduck:dinner:3"` vs `need` `"turtleduck:meal:<date>:dinner"`; a derived trip's `sched:<date>` is never a need id. `assign` matches a slot only on the need's own date.
- **15-minute grid:** Turtleduck validates every time on it, else a 07:40 breakfast would pin at 07:45 and the apps disagree.
- **Ticks on a shared day:** the accepted edge, documented.
- **Older copies** drop the new keys and fields when they save; both changelogs say reload on every device; a reloaded copy re-derives trips and re-syncs slots.
- **Phone:** Momo — the static pin glyph keeps fixed cards readable; Turtleduck — Settings is the way to Times & trips (the ⋯ menu is desktop-only), Confirm and the status sit in the list's week heads, the Time row reuses `entry-step`, the trip time lives in the Groceries header.
- **Pinned cards clash** with cards above that run past them: Momo's existing `timeTrouble` flag; expected — the owner reshapes the baseline once (Fill gaps gives Free time before each pinned card).
